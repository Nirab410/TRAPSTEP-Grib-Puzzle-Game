How to Play TRAPSTEP

🎯 Objective:

Move your avatar from the green START tile to the golden ★ GOAL tile.

⚡ Trail Collapse:

Every tile you step OFF crumbles permanently into the abyss! You cannot step on collapsed tiles or static walls.

👥 The Shadow Doppelgänger:

Your holographic clone moves in mirror reflection to you! Both of you collapse tiles behind you. If your clone hits a wall, falls into a void, or touches a laser, you FAIL!

❄️ Ice Momentum (Slide):

Stepping on ice slides you until hitting a wall, collapsing every ice tile you slide through in a chain reaction!

🌀 Quantum Wormholes (Portals):

Entering Portal α instantly teleports you to Portal β (and vice versa). The entrance crumbles upon exit.

⚡ Laser Gates & Switches:

Deadly laser grids block passages. Step on 🔘 SWITCH plates (or have your clone step on them) to toggle lasers on/off.
